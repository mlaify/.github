# Support and Reporting

M&L AI is run by volunteers. We do our best to support all of our projects, but support is best
effort: there is no response-time commitment, and an issue may be closed or receive no individual
response. M&L AI is small: it is not a large company with dedicated teams for security, support,
and everything else.

Unless a repository explicitly says otherwise, maintainer development and testing are on macOS.
Linux support is limited to what that repository documents, and Windows is unsupported.

Choose the channel that matches the problem:

- **Bug, crash, installation problem, compatibility issue, or incorrect result:** open a public
  issue in the affected repository and use its issue form.
- **Feature request or documentation correction:** use the matching public issue form.
- **Confidential vulnerability in the project itself:** use the affected repository's
  **Security** tab and select **Report a vulnerability**.
- **Privacy request:** use the public [privacy request form](https://github.com/mlaify/mlaify.io/issues/new?template=privacy.yml)
  without personal information; see the [privacy policy](https://mlaify.io/privacy-policy/).

Before posting logs, screenshots, reports, or sample projects, remove secrets, credentials,
personal information, proprietary source code, and third-party data.

The organization-wide [security reporting policy](SECURITY.md) explains scope and reporting
limits. There is no bug bounty or paid disclosure program, and no payment, rewards, or swag are
offered.
